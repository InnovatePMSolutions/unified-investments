What We Do

Comprehensive Field Services

From emergency winterizations to full make-ready turnovers, our certified field crews handle every phase of the property preservation lifecycle — all under one vendor relationship.

Winterizations

Full HUD/FHA-compliant winterization protocols including anti-freeze treatment of all water supply and drain lines, water heater draining, shut-off valve activation, and pressure testing. We issue photographic proof-of-completion packets for every asset on submission day.

Compliance Ready

Grass Cuts

Recurring lawn maintenance across REO, occupied, and vacant properties. Services include front/rear mowing, edging, trimming, debris bag-out, and before/after photographic documentation submitted directly to your work order platform within 24 hours of completion.

Recurring Programs Available

Trashouts

Full interior and exterior debris removal operations. Our crews perform complete room-by-room cleanouts, furniture and appliance haul-away, yard debris clearing, and licensed disposal at certified transfer stations. Cubic yardage estimates provided upfront for accurate billing.

Licensed Disposal

Roof Repairs

Emergency tarping, shingle replacement, flashing repair, soffit and fascia restoration, and gutter reattachment. Every roof assignment is assessed with drone-assisted or manual inspection, and we deliver a detailed repair scope with photo evidence prior to work order authorization.

Inspection Reports Included

Low-Voltage Electrical

Maintenance-level electrical servicing including outlet/switch replacement, GFCI installation, circuit breaker resets, smoke and CO detector installation, doorbell systems, exterior security lighting, and panel labeling for REO compliance audits. All work performed under applicable local ordinance thresholds.

Code Compliant

HVAC

Furnace and central air unit inspections, filter replacements, condensate line clearing, thermostat commissioning, and heat-season operability certification. We identify and document deferred maintenance items and provide competitive repair bids for serviceable vs. replace-level equipment assessments.

Operability Certified

Eviction Services

Coordinated lock-out support in conjunction with court-ordered eviction enforcement. Our field teams stand by for same-day deployment alongside sheriff deputies, perform immediate post-eviction property securing (re-key, board-up as needed), and execute a full condition report at time of possession.

Same-Day Mobilization

Initial / Make-Ready

End-to-end property condition assessments and turn-key make-ready preparation. Services include initial inspection reports with photographic inventories, interior cleaning, re-keying, paint touch-up, minor drywall repair, and final punch-list verification — delivering investor-grade asset readiness on your timeline.

Full Turn-Key Available
